# Margin-anchored square picture placement

For an object-only body paragraph containing a picture anchored to both page margins with square wrapping on both sides, EdgeDoc now retains the paragraph and positions the picture relative to the margin rectangle. Previously it replaced that paragraph with an image block, shifted the image, and altered the spacing of subsequent content. This is a bounded placement improvement: exclusion regions and text wrapping around the picture are not implemented, and the unsupported-floating-picture diagnostic remains in place.

The pinned six-page ASIC document `418b154e5c` has an object-only logo anchored at the top center of the margins. Before this change, the first-page heading was 26.4 points too low and pages 1–2 failed the visual comparison. Afterward, all six pages match the Wordcraft text, glyph positions, and raster gate with zero unmatched ink; the first page was inspected in EdgeDoc, Wordcraft, and its Word-exported PDF. The document remains a strict failure because square wrapping and a field type are still diagnosed. The input is identified by `tests/corpus/word-benchmark-manifest.json`.

A focused test checks margin position, preservation of the anchor paragraph, and the retained diagnostic. The 48-document benchmark recorded 12 passes, 34 failures, and 2 blocked reference timeouts, while all 209 selected previous passes passed again. Exact-source results are in `word-benchmark-48-margin-square-summary.json` and `margin-square-regression-209-summary.json`. The workspace passed 412 tests and strict Clippy; the rebuilt browser passed 18 interaction checks and 134 checks across 33 documents.
